## A23-07 — wallet-journal runway
- A wallet journal holds exactly one attempt (`reserve` refuses once anything is saved:
  `ONE_SHOT_ALREADY_CONSUMED_OR_UNKNOWN`); a waiting UNKNOWN does not grow it (observations persist
  only on change). The earlier estimate "≈ 2 400 attempts" does not apply.
- Measured with the production writer (test `a23_07_wallet_journal_runway_is_measured_and_stays_reopenable`):
  settled attempt 8 052 B; not-landed exits of its position grow quadratically (every version carries
  all exits): first exit 12 002 B, 88th 339 934 B; 88 exits = 15 487 336 B of 16 777 216.
- Fix: `reserve_exit` refuses a new exit (`JOURNAL_RUNWAY_EXHAUSTED`) unless 16 frames of its reserved
  version still fit under the reopen limit — before, the running journal could outgrow it and the next
  start would fail with `JOURNAL_SIZE_LIMIT` while holding the position. The refusal keeps the position
  held; the journal reopens with every exit. No truncation, no release, no rotation (not needed for
  the one-shot journal; a multi-attempt LIVE journal needs its own contract).
- Mutation fail-before: no lifecycle headroom (journal outgrows the reopen limit).
